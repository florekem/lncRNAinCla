Figure 1. **Schematic representation of pipeline used to profile changes in expression of putative long non-coding RNAs in MC-LR-induced liver damage in whitefish**

Figure 2. **Differentially expressed known non-coding RNAs after MC-LR exposure.** (A) Venn diagram of DE transcripts with homology to any transcript deposited in RFAM database. (B) Venn diagram of DE transcripts with homology to transcripts labeled as long non-coding in RFAM database.

Figure 3. **Differentially expressed pairs of putative autonomous 3' UTR and protein coding transcripts of the same mRNA after MC-LR exposure.** (A) Percentages of DE corresponding contigs of the same mRNA, that both were upregulated (red) or down-regulated (blue), as well as those which expression profiles were contradictory. (B) Percentages of corresponding pairs in which ratio of PCT to putative autonomous 3' UTR transcripts were higher in control (PBS) than in exposed (MC-LR) groups (brown), as well as those in which ratio was higher in exposed than in control (green). (C) Venn diagram of all PCT (paired with 3' UTR and unpaired) that expression was changed after MC-LR exposure. (D) Venn diagram of all PCT downregulated after MC-LR.

Figure 4. **Gene ontology of co-expressed pairs of putative autonomous 3' UTR and protein coding transcripts of the same mRNA after MC-LR exposure.** Percentage of co-upregulated (A) and co-downregulated (B) pairs of day 6 and day 9 involved in processes grouped in cellular component, molecular function and biological process. (C) Enriched GO terms of upregulated pairs of day 1 when compared with day 6 and day 9.

Figure 5. **Density distributions of coding and putative novel lncRNAs transcripts show differences in length-corrected minimum free energy, content of GC base pairs, and lengths of transcripts.** (A) Comparison of distribution of length-corrected minimum free energy. LncRNA transcripts show higher mean (−0.237 kcal/ mol/nt) than that of the protein-coding transcripts (−0.289 kcal/ mol/nt); (B) Comparison of distribution of content of GC base pairs. LncRNA transcripts show lower mean GC base pairs content (0.393) than that of protein-coding transcripts (0.460); (C) Comparison of distribution of lengths of transcripts. LncRNAs show shorter mean sequence length (949 bp) than that of protein-coding transcripts (1747 bp). 

Figure 6. **Number of differentially expressed novel long non-coding RNAs after MC-LR treatment presented as venn diagrams.** (A) up-regulated, (B) down-regulated. Transcripts overlapping between days after administering MC-LR distinguishes two groups, where transcripts from 1d does not overlap so well with other days, as transcripts from 6d and 9d do.
